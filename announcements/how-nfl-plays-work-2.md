**How to read a play**
`★ BUF -2.5 -105 @ Novig · EV +3.1% · bet 0.94% · fair -118 (consensus of 9 books) · Pinnacle EV +2.7%`
`good to -112 (EV +2.0%, bet 0.52%)`
- **EV**: expected return at that price vs the consensus fair line
- **bet**: suggested size as a % of your bankroll (1/8 Kelly, never more than 2%). Bigger edge = bigger bet.
- **Pinnacle EV**: the same play vs Pinnacle alone (both have to clear the bar)
- **good to**: the worst price still worth betting, with the EV and bet size at that price. Worse than that, pass.
