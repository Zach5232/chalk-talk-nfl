📣 **How NFL plays work in this channel**

**What makes a play a BET ★**
We don't just copy one book. For every spread, total and team total we build a **fair line from the whole market**: every book's price with the vig removed, weighted toward the sharpest sources (Pinnacle counts most, then the exchanges like Novig and ProphetX, then the regular books). A play only gets a ★ when the best available price beats that consensus **and** beats Pinnacle on its own by at least **+2%** (+3% for team totals). If only one of the two likes it, it's a "lean" and stays on the site, not in here.

**What you'll see**
📋 **Week card**: every ★ play for the week, grouped by game day, the first time odds load each week (usually Tuesday)
🆕 **New BET**: a ★ play that shows up later in the week
📈 **Better number**: a play we already posted got a better line or price (the old price is shown so you know if you already have the better one)
❌ **Off the board**: a play we posted is no longer a BET. If you haven't placed it yet, skip it.
☀️ **Still good today**: on game days, the ★ plays for that day's games at the best current price
📊 **Weekly recap**: every Tuesday, how the plays posted here did (W-L, units at 1u each, and how often we beat the closing line), plus the season record
